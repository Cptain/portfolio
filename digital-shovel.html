About Digital Shovel

Crypto Mining Infrastructure

Digital Shovel is a North American provider of revolutionary crypto mining containers designed for rapid deployment and maximum efficiency.

Their modular container solutions enable businesses and individuals to set up professional-grade mining operations anywhere in the world.

With a focus on innovation and performance, Digital Shovel bridges the gap between blockchain technology and practical mining infrastructure.

My Roles

UI/UX

I was responsible for the UI/UX design of Digital Shovel's website, including designing subtle animations for icons and backgrounds to bring more life to the interface. I designed isometric illustrations for each product, using callouts to highlight key features, and created interactive animations that "explode" products to reveal their internal parts. I also designed a set of humorous, custom emojis for the company's internal Slack.

Front-end

I wrote the code for the company's static website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and GSAP, and designed and developed an internal web application that allowed technologists to test PCBs, automatically validating pass/fail results against defined requirements. I also built responsive email templates in MJML for company newsletters, events, and product updates. Additionally, I added a contact form to the website, connected through Formspree, which became the first channel through which the company began receiving customer interest from the web.

Animation

I created pseudo-3D product visualizations using pure CSS to display our products without relying on 3D rendering or video. Using After Effects, I designed and animated product demonstration videos for events, tradeshows, and conferences, covering scenarios such as product assembly, loading and delivery, airflow recirculation, concrete pouring, crane lifting, and busway assembly and delivery. I also designed and animated marketing promos for our products.

Branding

I built a full design system, including components, icons, and logos, to support Digital Shovel and its sister brands. I designed the Digital Shovel brand identity, including a 5-year anniversary logo and the AWS Test Bench product logo, along with brand identities for three sister brands: Good Pocket Studios, BluAX, and The Sailing Beard. I designed product packaging for BluAX (solo miner), branded office labels, and each employee's company email signature.

Marketing

I designed animated and static online ads, 360° video overlays, banners, cards, flags, flyers, headers for brand collaborations, signs, slideshows, and tablecloths for events and campaigns. I designed product manuals and branded food and drink menus for company events, along with tradeshow booth mockups, and handled product photography as needed. I also designed and managed content for all of the company's social media posts, and put together a business deck used to request funding for a large company project.

Merchandise

I designed branded merchandise, including hats, hoodies, lanyards, mugs, pens, shaker bottles, socks, tote bags, t-shirts, cannabis grinders, and event wristbands, and managed procurement and manufacturing through China.

Colour Palette

The goal was to create a bold, trustworthy identity that communicates technological expertise and reliability — the core values of a mining infrastructure company.

Digital Blue takes centre stage as the primary brand colour, conveying confidence and innovation. The blue-gray accent adds vibrancy and energy while the white ensures legibility and balance across all surfaces.
