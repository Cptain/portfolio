About Vervendo

Online Booking Platform

A startup company building an innovative online booking platform to personalize and enhance travelling experiences.

Using a simple, smartly designed user-friendly reservation platform where travelers can access and interact with hoteliers to personalized custom-made deals based on the traveler's needs. It's focused on the Chania (Crete, Greece) market with plans for expanding to all of Crete.

Vervendo relieves travellers from doing all the legwork of searching and comparing prices and accommodations to they match their ideal needs.

Typography

Primary Font

As part of the brand refresh, we changed our font from Roboto to Manrope.

Manrope provided the flexibility of both latin and greek characters, which was a helpful asset for a greek company.

As a wider and cleaner font, it served our goal of creating a smooth and engaging environment for our users.

Secondary Font

To add some warmth, quirkiness, and excitement to our type, we included a complimentary font with a handwritten/cursive style, named Caveat.

How Vervendo Works

Through a quick form the traveller can personalize their vacations. Their request is filtered and sent to all matching hosts.

Hosts respond to travelers' requests by creating unique offers that include price discounts, services, experiences, and a note with any additional details.

The travellers get notified with every unique offer received and can compare them easily through their dashboard before deciding which one to choose.
