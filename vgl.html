About VGL eSports

Tournament & League Gaming Platform

VGL eSports (Virtual Gaming Leagues) is a platform dedicated to organizing and managing esports tournaments, leagues, and events.

They focus on creating a comprehensive ecosystem for gamers, teams, and partners, while also helping players improve their skills through our eSports Academy.

They participated in the Digital Expo, back in 2019, in Athens, Greece. The event managed to bring together over 20,000 fans of esports and technology.

Colour Palette

The goal was to add a third color to the palette to enhance the energetic atmosphere that fits esports and our competitive environment.

With energy as my focus, I decided to build the theme around the colors of the sun. The yellow was paired with a new reddish orange, while also utilizing radial gradients to further add to the concept.

Working with games of the sports genre, required displaying data visually almost constantly. Consequently, typography was the main challenge. The task was to find a family that offered an extensive selection of weights to ensure flexibility.

The answer was 'Montserrat', offering a myriad of different weights to work.

Wide and smooth characters paired well with the tall and condensed secondary font, as well as with our vibrant colours.
